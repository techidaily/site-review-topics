"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Apple iPhone SE (2020) device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Apple iPhone SE (2020) device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.

"Location Not Available" means that the Apple iPhone SE (2020) device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Apple iPhone SE (2020) device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.
