"Location Not Available" means that the Samsung Galaxy S23 Tactical Edition device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Samsung Galaxy S23 Tactical Edition device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.

In summary, "No Location Found" means the app could not locate the Samsung Galaxy S23 Tactical Edition device, while "Location Not Available" means that the Samsung Galaxy S23 Tactical Edition device is not currently sharing its location.
