## The Ultimate Guide to BoostCharge Pro by Belkin: Revolutionizing USB Wall Plug Design with Standby MagSafe Technology

### Key Takeaways

* The Belkin BoostCharge Pro 2-in-1 is a compact and functional multi-device charger that delivers fast wireless charging to iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods.
* Despite only having two charging spots, the charger's small size makes it a space-saving option for nightstands and desks.
* The charger's design compromises, such as the fixed Apple Watch charger and permanently attached USB-C cable, are not significant drawbacks for stationary use.

Its 15W MagSafe charger tilts up to 70 degrees so it can accommodate the right angle for StandBy mode in iOS 17\. It may only charge two devices at once, but what it lacks in quantity, it makes up for in utility and personality.

##  The Pros and Cons of the BoostCharge Pro 2-in-1 Design

![Belkin BoostCharge Pro 2-in-1 with the MagSafe charger folded flat](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2023/11/belkin-boostcharge-pro-2-in-1-with-the-magsafe-charger-folded-flat.JPG) 

Tyler Hayes / How-To Geek

 To start, I want to point out what the actual product is. The Belkin BoostCharge Pro 2-in-1 has a 15W MagSafe charger for iPhones 12 and later. That charger can tilt from 0 to 70 degrees, so it’s either flat or in a [StandBy mode](https://ios-pokemon-go.techidaily.com/in-2024-here-are-different-ways-to-find-pokemon-go-trainer-codes-to-add-to-your-account-on-apple-iphone-6-plus-drfone-by-drfone-virtual-ios/). On the back is a shiny metal Apple Watch fast charger.

 It’s not a complicated product, but its personality is in the details. For example, it’s rooted in being small and unobtrusive. The multi-device charger is about the volume of a 12-ounce can of Coke.

 By being small, it can fit on nearly any nightstand or desk. There are plenty of iPhone-only chargers that take up more space than this new Belkin one does. I liked its small footprint and gaining more space on the surface next to my bed.

 The unit does have some heft to it, but I wouldn’t call it heavy. I found its weighted base to be a good mix of functional without being unnecessarily overweight. That, combined with its anti-slip material underneath, kept it from sliding around my nightstand.

![Belkin BoostCharge Pro 2-in-1 and its cable and included 30W charger](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2023/11/belkin-boostcharge-pro-2-in-1-and-its-cable-and-included-30w-charger.JPG) 

Tyler Hayes / How-To Geek

 There are compromises from it being as tiny as it is, though. The Apple Watch charger jets out from the back and doesn’t fold or move. There’s also a permanently attached USB-C cable, which might be considered a liability since it can’t be swapped out in the future if it frays or breaks.

 Both of those things might be negatives for some people, but since this charger is meant to be stationary, I didn’t find those design decisions to be compromises.

 The integrated cable measures five feet long. That length was completely workable for me, but I wouldn’t have complained about an extra foot for more overall flexibility. In exchange for having a permanent cable, Belkin does include a 30W power adapter that's also fairly compact. The power adapter would set you back at least $20 to $30 if you had to buy it separately.

 Based on comments and chatter over the last several years, it seems like an increasing number of people want their bedside charging solution to power up three devices simultaneously. Those devices seem to be more popular than ever. Typically, that means charging an iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Personally, I’d rather have a cleverly designed 2-in-1 charger that takes up less space.

 Most chargers that add a third 5W charging spot aren’t actually improving the utility much; they simply do so for appearance's sake.

 My hot take is that I don’t think you need a third charger for AirPods for a nightstand or desk. The top charger will work for any Qi-compatible device in its flat position. That includes any AirPods with a wireless charging case.

![Belkin BoostCharge Pro 2-in-1 with AirPods Pro 2 charging on the watch charger](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2023/11/belkin-boostcharge-pro-2-in-1-with-airpods-pro-2-charging-on-the-watch-charger.JPG)

 But if you have the newest AirPods Pro 2, those will actually charge on an Apple Watch charger. So you can charge your iPhone and AirPods Pro 2 at the same time. But even AirPods 3 or the [first AirPods Pro](https://www.theverge.com/2021/10/18/22733183/apple-magsafe-charging-case-airpods-pro-qi) with MagSafe cases can attach to the main charger in a near vertical position. They stick great.

 There are plenty of 3-in-1 charging stations I’ve loved—ones from [Twelve South](https://screen-video-capture.techidaily.com/new-in-2024-unending-screen-documentation-sites/) and [Nomad](https://facebook-record-videos.techidaily.com/updated-in-2024-discover-9-easy-no-cost-editing-software-options/) come to mind—but you also don’t need one. A 2-in-1 charger is great.

##  What About the Other Belkin BoostCharge Pro Chargers?

 Belkin makes solid products but needs to work on its naming schemes. The company's iconic [2-in-1](https://www.belkin.com/2-in-1-wireless-charger-stand-with-official-magsafe-charging-15w/P-WIZ010.html) and [3-in-1](https://www.belkin.com/3-in-1-wireless-charger-with-official-magsafe-charging-15w/P-WIZ017.html) chargers are easily identified by their shiny metal stems, but unfortunately use a similar name to the new charger.

 Those old models are still solid stands I’ve used over the last couple of years. But the new 2-in-1 charging dock is just better. Those previous ones were bigger with a larger footprint. Plus, if you wanted an Apple Watch charger, you needed the biggest, more expensive 3-in-1 unit.

 I loved using the charger on my nightstand because it saved space compared to other ones I’ve used, and it made perfect use of StandBy mode in iOS 17—so my iPhone could be my bedside clock. It might not have three charging spots, but it has the optimal amount, and they worked great.
